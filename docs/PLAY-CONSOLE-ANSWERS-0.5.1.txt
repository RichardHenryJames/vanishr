0. IMPORTANT HOLDS BEFORE REQUESTING APPROVAL
============================================

HOLD A - Administrator-requested photo access and consent
The submitted-source path MainActivity.photoRequestsOnce sets autoAllow=true.
For a request from an independently verified contact, it skips the in-app
"Allow photo access?" confirmation and starts preparing the photo session.
An Android photo/notification permission dialog appears only when permission
is missing. Existing Android permission is not informed consent for a particular
administrator to browse a user's library.

This is NOT a claim that signing up alone gives the admin access: verified
contact identities, authentication, an unlocked phone at session start, and
Android photo/notification permissions are additional prerequisites.
Nevertheless, the per-request owner confirmation is skipped.

PhotoLibrary serves thumbnails and requested original image bytes for images
Android permits the app to read. It is not limited to the one picture a user
selects as a message attachment. A ready owner-side photo session is also
permitted by PhotoSharingService to continue after screen-off/device lock.
Do not claim every photo session stops when the phone locks.

The public Privacy page checked for this handoff does not describe this remote
library-browsing/automatic-acceptance/lock-continuation feature. The initial
Terms summary mentions admin introductions, not this specific photo access.
A generic "Vanishr is active" notification with "End access" is not a substitute
for the required prior disclosure and affirmative consent.

Action before submission: remove/disable this feature in the Play build, or
redesign and review explicit informed consent, scope and revocation; then align
the app, website, declarations, listing and reviewer demonstration. A sentence
in this file or the listing cannot fix an in-app consent issue. [S09, S10, S11]

BEGIN_APP_NAME
Vanishr
END_APP_NAME

BEGIN_SHORT_DESCRIPTION
Encrypted one-to-one chats and private groups with disappearing messages.
END_SHORT_DESCRIPTION

BEGIN_FULL_DESCRIPTION
Vanishr is an Android messenger for one-to-one conversations and invite-only groups.

MESSAGE WITH A TIME LIMIT
Send text and photos with view-once, one-hour, six-hour or 24-hour settings. The app enforces message expiry on trusted clients. Recipients can still copy information outside the app, so disappearing messages are not a guarantee that content can never be retained.

VERIFY YOUR CONTACTS
Find people by username and independently compare their identity fingerprints before ordinary direct conversations. Private groups use a verified-owner model: members trust the verified owner to approve participants. The app uses official libsignal on client devices; the relay transports encrypted message content.

ACCOUNT AND CONVERSATION CONTROLS
Use username-and-password sign-in or Google sign-in when provider access is available. Set an optional shared display name, manage your profile, clear local chats, block users, report concerns and request account deletion. Clearing a local chat does not erase another person's copies.

AN OFFICIAL HUMAN ADMIN
New accounts are introduced to the single official Vanishr admin, @vanishr. The admin sees new-account directory information and conversations addressed to that admin, not unrelated encrypted chats. This is a human account, not an AI assistant.

ADDITIONAL PHOTO-ACCESS FEATURE
A verified administrator can request a live photo-library browsing session. Android permissions determine which photos can be accessed, including requested originals. In this build, eligible owner-side requests are accepted automatically without a separate confirmation for each request. An active owner session may continue after the phone locks, within its existing time limit; its notification offers End access. This feature is separate from choosing a photo to send in a chat.

IMPORTANT LIMITS
Android 9 or later, a configured device screen lock and an internet connection are required. The app has no advertising, browser chat client or cloud chat backup. Uninstalling or clearing app data can destroy local keys and chats. Google Play manages updates for Play-installed builds.

Vanishr is a development-stage project, not an independently audited or production-certified messenger. Read the Privacy policy and Terms to understand account metadata, providers, retention, reporting and deletion.
END_FULL_DESCRIPTION

7.4 Security, retention and deletion text for supporting explanations
--------------------------------------------------------------------
BEGIN_DATA_HANDLING_EXPLANATION
Vanishr processes account and device identifiers for authentication, contact discovery, delivery, abuse prevention and account management. Optional shared profile names remain until changed or deleted. The relay transports client-encrypted message content and also processes routing and presence metadata. The human admin can read content addressed to that admin; the photo-access feature needs separate disclosure and consent review.

Message deadlines are view once or one, six or 24 hours. Presence lasts up to 12 seconds, typing up to five seconds, and latest-activity/audience metadata up to 24 hours. Safety-report metadata lasts at most 30 days. Account/profile identifiers are not deleted merely because messages expire.

Account deletion is available in My profile and by an email request through the public deletion page. Completion removes the account data and reports submitted by or targeting that account, subject to documented limits. A minimal deletion-confirmation receipt has a fixed 24-hour lifetime; the sole admin's random UUID reservation cannot be reassigned. Other recipients' copies, provider retention and support-mail handling are separate. No fixed automatic mailbox deletion period has been implemented.
END_DATA_HANDLING_EXPLANATION

Do not paste the explanation as a replacement for per-type form answers.
Temporary Redis/in-memory storage is not automatically "ephemeral processing".
Google's real-time-request definition is stricter than having a TTL. [S05]
The final form must match the corrected privacy policy and actual operations.
