A LITTLE LESS PERMANENT

A conversation, not an archive.

Vanishr is an Android app for one-to-one chats and invite-only groups. Send text and photos, set their lifetime, and keep private keys on your device.

Choose what lasts

View-once messages or a one-hour, six-hour or 24-hour deadline. Expiry is bounded on the client and relay, even when a recipient stays offline.

Encrypted is a design.

The client uses official libsignal for direct sessions and group sender keys. The relay routes ciphertext and does not hold message-decryption keys.

Security model and limitations

Device-owned keysAndroid Keystore protects local encrypted state. A phone screen lock is required.

A FEW STRAIGHT ANSWERS

About Vanishr

What is Vanishr?

Vanishr is an open-source Android messenger for end-to-end encrypted, disappearing text and photos in direct chats and invite-only private groups. This website distributes its signed development APK and corresponding source.

Is Vanishr free?

The development APK and source downloads are free. Vanishr is distributed under the GNU AGPL version 3, without warranty. Third-party components retain their own licenses.

Does it work on iPhone or in a browser?

No. The current native app requires Android 9 or later and a configured phone screen lock. This website is an information and download site, not a browser chat client.

Can everyone see my profile photo?

No. Username search does not show photos. Both people must add and independently verify each other as contacts before the trusted clients exchange encrypted profile photos. Group membership alone does not grant access.

Can a disappearing message still be saved?

Yes. A recipient can photograph a screen, modify their client, or otherwise retain content. Expiry and screenshot protections reduce accidental retention but cannot guarantee that every copy disappears.

Is Vanishr affiliated with Signal or Google?

No. Vanishr uses the official libsignal library for its encryption sessions and offers optional Google sign-in. It is not an official Signal or Google product, and those integrations are not an endorsement.
