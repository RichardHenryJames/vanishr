Vanishr / Website privacy

UPDATED 20 SEPTEMBER 2026

Your visit. Your choice.

This policy covers the public information and download website. It does not turn the website into a chat client or connect website visitors to private conversations.

This website is separate from the app

The Vanishr project operates this public site to explain the Android app and distribute release files. It does not offer account login, chat forms or a message viewer. Fonts, icons and product images are served from this site. Product screenshots contain fictional test data.

The Android app has no analytics SDK or advertising. This website's optional Google Analytics setting does not enable tracking inside the app. Read the app security model for how accounts, messages, metadata and local keys are handled.

Hosting and network information

The site is hosted by Vercel. Delivering a page or download necessarily exposes connection information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information and request time to hosting/network providers. Their operational processing is not controlled by the optional analytics switch.

There are no advertising embeds, social-media pixels or session-replay tools. Following a link to GitHub, Google or another external service is governed by that service's own policy. This site uses a no-referrer policy for outgoing navigation.

Optional Google Analytics

__ANALYTICS_STATUS__

When configured, Google Analytics 4 loads only after you choose Allow. Before consent or after No thanks, the site does not load the Google tag or send Google Analytics requests, including cookieless analytics pings.

With consent, analytics measures page visits and clicks on the public APK/source download links. It can report approximate geography, browser/device categories, sessions and source websites. Google receives network information during these requests and may process data internationally under its terms.

We do not send app usernames, email addresses, contact IDs, chats, photos or encryption keys to analytics. Page URLs are sent without query strings or fragments; referrers are reduced to the source origin. Advertising storage, advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in the website tag. Consent-based audience data is incomplete by design because visitors can decline or block it.

See Google's privacy policy and Google Analytics data safeguards for the provider's processing.

Change your choice

The site stores your consent choice locally in this browser for up to 180 days. With analytics allowed, first-party _ga cookies may identify a browser for up to 180 days. These are not Vanishr account identifiers.

Use Cookie settings at any time to turn analytics off. Revoking consent removes the site's analytics cookies and reloads the page without the Google tag. It stops future collection in this browser; it cannot undo information already sent to Google. A Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal keeps analytics off. Declining does not restrict any page or download.

Website analytics is off until you choose otherwise.

Questions and updates

General website or application issues can be raised through the Vanishr project issue tracker. Do not put personal information, private conversation details or credentials in a public issue.

This policy will be updated when site measurement or hosting practices change. App releases and this website remain development software, not an independently reviewed security service.
