Private does not mean permissionless.

Using Vanishr

Use an account you control, protect your sign-in credentials and respect other people's privacy and decisions not to communicate. You are responsible for what you send and for having permission to share it. Read the Privacy policy and security limitations before sharing.

Vanishr is an independent open-source development project, not an emergency service, a continuously monitored safety service, an independently audited messenger or a promise of Google Play approval. Do not use this development build for real sensitive conversations yet. These standards do not claim child-directed certification or compliance with every jurisdiction's age and privacy laws.

The app's source is under the GNU AGPL version 3; third-party software retains its own licenses. These service rules do not replace those software licenses.

Unacceptable content and conduct

Do not use Vanishr to create, request, send, coordinate, promote or facilitate:

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation, grooming, child sexual abuse material, or any sexual exploitation of a person under 18.
  • Threats, stalking, harassment, hate or targeted abuse, coercion, sexual exploitation, or encouragement of violence.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery, disclosure of someone else's private information, or sharing photos without the necessary permission.
  • Impersonation, fraud, phishing, spam, malware, or attempts to obtain another person's password, token or private encryption key.
  • Unauthorized account/device/gallery access, evasion of a block, bypassing Android permissions, or covert surveillance.
  • Other unlawful content or activity, including infringement of another person's rights.

A group owner must not invite or retain participants to facilitate prohibited conduct. Do not misrepresent identity verification, an admin label or disappearing-message settings as proof of consent or a guarantee of safety.

Child sexual abuse and exploitation prevention

Vanishr prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This includes grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, sextortion, trafficking for sexual exploitation, and the creation, possession for distribution, exchange or promotion of sexual-abuse imagery involving minors, including generated imagery.

Do not request, download, forward or email suspected CSAM to prove a report. Use the child-safety report category where available or contact richardhenryjames64@gmail.com with subject Vanishr child safety concern. Send only the necessary account/report identifiers, selected concern and optional message/group identifiers, not the content itself.

The operator's responsibility under these standards is to assess reports, take appropriate action on substantiated exploitation, restrict responsible accounts where warranted, and report suspected child exploitation to the appropriate national or regional child-protection or law-enforcement authority where legally required. The contact above is the public escalation route for child-safety concerns and policy questions.

The relay cannot read end-to-end encrypted conversations or proactively inspect their images. An optional report does not upload plaintext evidence. Human review and appropriate legal escalation are required; publishing these rules does not establish an always-staffed moderation operation or guarantee that abuse will be detected. If a child or anyone else is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency or child-protection service; do not wait for an email reply.

Block a user or report a concern

Account and safety controls: version matters.

__ACCOUNT_CONTROLS_NOTE__

The 0.4.7 controls provide Block user and Report user in the direct-conversation menu, with a Report message action for content. Choose the relevant reason and review the confirmation before submitting. You may report without sending text, photos or screenshots to the operator. Blocking and reporting are separate choices.

Reports contain a random report ID, reporter and target account IDs, reason, submission/expiry metadata and optional message/group IDs. Accepted reports enter an admin moderation queue for human review, with a maximum 30-day lifetime from submission. Completed account deletion removes reports submitted by or targeting that account, without a safety-retention exception. Deleting the pinned admin clears the entire undeliverable report queue. A report is not a message-content upload or automatic sanction. See report privacy and retention.

If there is no enabled, pinned official admin account, the relay rejects new in-app reports with 503 safety_review_unavailable. This is not successful submission and does not imply a moderation service is available. Use the email contact below for concerns; neither an enabled admin account nor that mailbox guarantees continuous human monitoring.

Blocking stops new direct interaction on the updated service; it is not remote erasure or a guarantee of separation in a shared group. Existing group contexts and already delivered copies may remain. Leave the group or ask its owner to act if you do not want shared participation.

If a control is unavailable, if the concern involves the admin, or if you want to follow up, email richardhenryjames64@gmail.com with subject Vanishr safety concern. The admin account and this operator contact are not an independent appeals body. Do not post personal details to public GitHub issues. Do not send credentials, private conversations or illegal images by email.

Handling violations and account choices

Violations may lead to operator-imposed account or service restrictions or removal, based on available information and applicable requirements. Human handling is necessary; no instant response, fixed support SLA or guaranteed moderation outcome is promised. The service does not monitor unrelated conversations, and an admin role does not supply their decryption keys.

Contact richardhenryjames64@gmail.com to ask about a report or restriction. Share only necessary identifiers, not private content. The operator should review mistakes and explain next steps where safe to do so; this does not promise an independent appeals service.

You can stop using the service and request deletion of your account. Signing out or uninstalling is not server account deletion. Initial deletion requires fresh account-only authentication. Before requesting it, the app must save a random deletion proof and the original UUID in encrypted local storage. If cleanup leaves the account in DELETING or the completion response is lost, that proof permits status checks and retry of the already-started deletion without a bearer sign-in token, not normal account use or deletion of another account. Local account data remains until explicit DELETED confirmation matches the original UUID; 401/404 errors are not confirmation.

A minimal deletion receipt containing only the proof digest, original random UUID, state and timestamps has a fixed 24-hour lifetime from creation and survives account deletion. Access expires at the deadline without extension by checks/retries; an every-minute purge removes expired rows while the service runs. See the retention policy for fields and physical-removal limits. This is not a safety-report retention exception: completed deletion removes reports submitted by or targeting your account. Previously received content copies follow their original expiry, and malicious recipients may keep them.
