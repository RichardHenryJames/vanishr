Install or update

  1. Download the APK from this page on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager. Review the source and permission request yourself.
  3. Install over an existing signed Vanishr release. Do not uninstall or clear app storage to update. Those actions delete local encryption keys and retained chats.
  4. Open Vanishr while the phone is unlocked and sign in or create an account. A release-signed APK cannot update a debug-signed installation.

Future update prompts appear at most once per day. You can also use My profile > Check for updates. Every update requires Android's installation approval; executable code is not silently replaced.

Your first verified conversation

  1. Share your username with the person you want to contact, and add their username.
  2. Compare the displayed safety numbers through an independent trusted channel, such as an in-person conversation. Do not rely on messages through this same relay for verification.
  3. Confirm the identity, then send a text or photo. Choose view once, one hour, six hours or 24 hours.

Your optional profile photo is only exchanged after both people add and independently verify one another. A shared name or photo is not a substitute for safety-number verification.

What's in 0.4.2

  • Clear chat appears after Remove contact in a direct chat's three-dot menu. It removes local messages and unsent items after confirmation, keeping the contact, verified identity and the other person's copies.
  • Administrators can request Remote Photos from the Photos menu. The other owner must choose Allow, with Android photo and notification permissions. Non-admin accounts cannot start a request.
  • Browse accessible photos as a scrollable thumbnail gallery, then load only the original you open. There is no gallery-count cap or whole-gallery upload.

Approved photo sharing can continue while the owner uses another app, with a persistent End access notification. Sessions last at most 15 minutes and end on phone lock, sign-out, permission loss, revocation or a disconnected photo channel. Android force-stop and background restrictions still apply. Both phones need 0.4.2. See Remote Photos security and limits.

Install over the existing app without uninstalling or clearing storage. Biometric-compatible opening, contact-photo fixes, remembered sign-in, private groups, notification preferences and Online/Typing/Last seen are retained.

One-time storage upgrade

An existing protected key may still need one phone PIN, pattern or password unlock to migrate. Lock the phone, unlock with that credential, then reopen Vanishr. Later opens can use any unlock method Android accepts. Android itself can still require a credential after reboot or biometric lockout.

On Android 14 and below, the app enforces the unlocked-phone boundary; new Android 15+ keys also retain Keystore lock enforcement. See the storage security tradeoff and Android's documented Keystore limitations. Physical-phone face and fingerprint behavior remains unverified.
